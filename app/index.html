Every trophy's name, description and grade is the official PlayStation 5 list, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles (the two agree on all 55); tracked targets are the counts PlayStation tracks, as listed by PSNProfiles. Each guide is written for this entry, in its own words, from the official description, and says plainly where a trigger is not yet verified in the game. Missable flags are cautions read from the official descriptions, not confirmed missables; the game is new and the flags say so.
